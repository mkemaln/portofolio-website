Offline-first note taking for field teams, syncing reliably on poor
connections.

## Problem

Field teams work in places with no usable connection, then lose hours
reconciling notes written in three different apps.

## Approach

- Local-first store with a conflict-free merge on reconnect.
- Progressive web app so it installs without an app store.
- Photo and voice capture that queue and upload opportunistically.

## Result

- **Zero data loss** across a six-month pilot with four field teams.
- Sync backlog clears within 90 seconds of regaining signal.

## Stack

`Svelte` `Supabase` `PWA`
