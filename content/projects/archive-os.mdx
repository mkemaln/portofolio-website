A personal knowledge system for researchers, built around fast full-text
search.

## Problem

Researchers accumulate thousands of documents with no reliable way to search
across them locally, and no trust that a hosted tool will keep them.

## Approach

- Built an index that stays local and syncs incrementally.
- Full-text search with ranked snippets, sub-50ms on a 40k-document corpus.
- Everything stored as plain files so the archive outlives the app.

## Result

- **40k documents** searchable in under 50ms.
- Used daily by a small cohort of researchers and writers.

## Stack

`TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Node.js`
