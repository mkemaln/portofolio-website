Headless storefront with edge rendering and a checkout that converts 18%
better.

## Problem

A legacy storefront with a 3.4s median load and a five-step checkout was losing
most of its mobile traffic before the cart.

## Approach

- Rebuilt the storefront headless on **Next.js with edge rendering**.
- Collapsed the checkout into a single page with inline validation.
- Moved product data behind a cache-first read model at the edge.

## Result

- Median load **3.4s → 680ms**.
- Checkout conversion **+18%**.
- Mobile revenue up **27%** quarter over quarter.

## Stack

`Next.js` `Stripe` `Edge Functions` `PostgreSQL`
