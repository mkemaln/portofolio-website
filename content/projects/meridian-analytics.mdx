A real-time analytics platform with composable widgets and a fully themable
charting layer.

## Problem

Product teams were stitching together screenshots and CSVs to answer questions
the analytics tool could not. The existing surface was fast to render but
impossible to extend.

## Approach

- Built a **composable widget grid** so teams could assemble their own dashboards.
- Wrote a charting layer with a single token contract, so theming is a config change.
- Streamed updates over a websocket with diff-based re-renders.

## Result

- Adoption by **9 of 11 internal teams** within two quarters.
- Time-to-first-dashboard dropped from days to under an hour.

## Stack

`React` `TypeScript` `D3` `WebSockets`
